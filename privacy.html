Privacy Policy

How VividFlow handles information when you use the Android application.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. About VividFlow

VividFlow is an Android media player developed by Kael Labs. It is designed to play user-selected media from compatible URLs, local files, and Android content sources.

2. Media Playback and Local Data

VividFlow may store playback-related information locally on your device to provide normal player functionality.

  • Playback position and progress
  • Player preferences
  • Application settings
  • Media references or URLs needed for playback

VividFlow does not operate a first-party cloud service for storing your media files or playback history.

3. Remote Media Sources

When you choose to play media hosted by another website or server, your device connects directly to that service. The external service may receive information normally required for network communication, such as your IP address, request headers, and connection details.

VividFlow does not control the privacy practices of third-party media providers.

4. Third-Party Services

VividFlow may include third-party services used for advertising, consent management, purchases, diagnostics, or application functionality. The current release may use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), AppLovin through Google Mobile Ads mediation, Meta Audience Network through Google Mobile Ads mediation, and Google Play Billing.

These providers may process information such as:

  • Device and app information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address and network information
  • App interaction information
  • Diagnostic or crash information
  • Purchase and transaction information

Third-party services process data according to their own privacy policies and applicable Google Play requirements.

5. Advertising

If advertising is enabled in the version of VividFlow you use, Google Mobile Ads and mediation partners such as AppLovin and Meta Audience Network may process device, network, advertising, and interaction information to deliver, measure, secure, and manage advertisements. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may be used to request and manage consent choices where required.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

VividFlow may offer purchases or subscriptions through Google Play. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. VividFlow does not directly receive or store your payment card details.

7. Permissions

VividFlow requests only Android permissions required for features available in the app, such as network access and access to media you explicitly choose to open. Permission availability may vary by Android version and enabled features.

8. Children's Privacy

VividFlow is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been collected improperly, contact us using the address below.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to limit unnecessary access to data and to keep application functionality secure. However, no software, device, or network communication method can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when VividFlow's functionality, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL and the “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.
